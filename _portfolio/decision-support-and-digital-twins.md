collection: portfolio

Developed a decision-support system for energy optimization in wastewater treatment plants. The work included time-series forecasting with ARIMA and LSTM models, and optimization of pumping schedules using genetic algorithms and particle swarm optimization.

Related work included identifying enhancements needed to evolve the decision-support system into a digital twin and applying reinforcement learning to maintenance scheduling for solar-energy systems.
