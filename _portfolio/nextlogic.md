**Roles: Task leader and technical contributor**

NEXTLOGIC is a Horizon Europe project supporting cities in their transition to climate neutrality by rethinking how urban space, mobility, and logistics are planned and managed.

The project develops a collaborative digital platform that enables cities to simulate, test, and optimise solutions before implementing them in the real world. It combines real-time data, geospatial analytics, and immersive visual tools to support decisions for businesses, citizens, and the environment.

The project brings together cities, logistics operators, local authorities, and citizens to co-design solutions such as:

- Zero-emission urban microhubs
- Smart loading and unloading zones
- Fifteen-minute multimodal hubs
- Incentive-based behavioural change mechanisms


**Task leader for T2.4: Digital ToolBox**
Objective: create a toolbox able to integrate several digital twins for co-design of mobility solutions.
My role: Task coordination between partners, requirement definition, development coordination.


**Technical contributor for T3.4: Electrification**
Objective: create a Local Digital Twin to monitor EV charging station, forecast electricity demand and plan installation of new charging station.
Target cities: Le Hague, Bergamo, Differdange.
My role: Technical contributor for algorithm development.



More information is available on the [NEXTLOGIC project website](https://www.nextlogic-project.eu/).
