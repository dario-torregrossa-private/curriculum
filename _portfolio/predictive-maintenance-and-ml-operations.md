Developed a system to remotely monitor tire wear, including prediction, validation, drift assessment, monitoring, and connections to an end-user interface. The work also explored knowledge-informed machine learning so that models account for physical constraints.

Applied production machine-learning practices including experiment tracking and model versioning with MLflow, service decoupling, REST APIs, and interface contracts.
