**HelioBrain** is a startup focused on digital-twin technology for photovoltaic (PV) plants.

The platform supports:

- **Monitoring** of PV plant performance
- **Effect allocation**, identifying the source of lower-than-expected production
- **Alert triggering** when relevant issues or performance deviations are detected
- **Forecasting** production of next years
- **Preventive maintenance & maintenance optimization** 

More information is available on the [HelioBrain website](https://heliobrain.com/).

The experience ended in Sept 2025, due to financial difficulties.
